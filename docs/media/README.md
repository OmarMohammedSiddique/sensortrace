## What is real and what is illustrative

The video is rendered from the web application's own components: the logo, the four verdict badges,
the colour tokens, the Geist fonts and the correlation-surface drawing from the home page. The
soundtrack was synthesised for the video.

These parts are illustrative and do not come from a real case:

- the questioned photo (a drawn sunset), its file name `IMG_4021.jpg` and its dimensions;
- the peak-to-correlation energy of 1,642, chosen to sit in the range [VALIDATION.md](../../VALIDATION.md)
  reports for same-device trials;
- the struck-out "87%", an example of the kind of figure SensorTrace never reports.

The claim "280/280 same-device trials matched on held-out phones" is the measured result in
[docs/validation/vision_subset_v1_report.md](../validation/vision_subset_v1_report.md).

No real user data, hosts or credentials appear in the video.
